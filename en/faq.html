FAQ

Can't find your answer? Email picandfill@taegabi.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Are my original photos uploaded to a server?

No. Originals stay in your phone's photo library. The app saves only your notes and a pointer to each photo. Small copies are uploaded only when you share by link, and deleted when you stop sharing or the share period ends.

My photos look gray.

Pic&Fill loads original photos from your phone's photo library. A photo looks gray if you deleted it from your library or you're viewing on a different phone. If you turned on Google Drive backup (Plus), go to Settings → Google Drive backup → Connect this phone on the new phone to see your backed-up photos.

What happens to my records when I change phones?

Sign in with the same Google or Apple account and your records are all there. Photos show up on the new phone too if you have Google Drive backup (Plus), as above.

I signed in with Apple, but my records are missing.

If you chose Hide My Email when signing in with Apple, a new account separate from your Google account is created. Please sign in with the account that has your records (Google, for example).

How much can I do for free?

You can record up to 300 photos and 10 trips and share them by link. Watch an ad to add up to 30 photos per trip, and to use text styling and full-content sharing.

How do I cancel Plus?

Go to iPhone Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions → Pic&Fill Plus → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next billing date to avoid the next charge.

Are my records deleted if I cancel?

No. Your records stay. Your 10 most recent trips stay editable and the rest become read-only. Subscribe again to edit them all.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account and request a refund.

Restore Purchases says it's used by another account.

Plus works on the Pic&Fill account you were signed in to when you first paid. Please sign in to that account (Google or Apple) again. If you're not sure which one, email us the Apple Account email you paid with.

Can I stop a share link or hide locations?

Yes. On the share screen you set a password, the share period and whether to hide exact locations. Stop sharing anytime in Settings → Active share links; the shared photos are deleted too.

Can I send photos straight from the Photos app?

Yes. Select photos in the Photos app, tap Share → Pic&Fill, then choose Travel, Half or Daily. If it's not in the list, turn it on under 'More' at the right end of the share sheet. Videos can't be added.

The ad didn't show and it moved on.

If an ad can't load, we let you continue without waiting. Just keep going.

Can I export my records or delete my account?

Yes. Settings → Export my records gives you all your records as a file, and Settings → Delete account deletes your account and records at once. Originals in your photo library and your Google Drive backup are not deleted.

Is it available on Android?

It's on iPhone first. Android is coming.
