How Travel works.

A day in Florence.

  1. 01

    One line when you snap

    Snap and write one line. The photo knows the time and place.

  2. 02

    Fill in each place

    Title, purpose, rating, hashtags and story. Places are suggested from photo locations.

  3. 03

    A day becomes a trip

    Your places line up in time order, with weather and spending.

  4. 04

    Map and contents

    Look back with a map and contents, and share by link or PDF.

How Half works.

A week of Hayul, who just learned to walk.

  1. 01

    One line when you snap

    Shoot with the center camera and write a short note. Photos are grouped by date.

  2. 02

    A weekly summary on weekends

    Tap 'Weekly' at the bottom to open seven day slots. Drag photos into them.

  3. 03

    Fill the week

    'Fill by date' puts them all in at once. Star the special days.

  4. 04

    One page, done

    Weight, health and special notes come together on one screen. Share by link or PDF.

How Daily works.

A monstera you have been growing.

  1. 01

    Saved by date

    Photos and one-line notes pile up by date.

  2. 02

    Gather them in one note

    Pick a few photos and write a little. That's a daily note.

  3. 03

    Places, too

    Add a shop or spot to a note and open it on a map.

  4. 04

    Sort by category

    Gather notes by your own categories, like Gardening or Workout.

Questions.

More questions →

Are my original photos uploaded to a server?

No. Originals stay in your phone's photo library. The app saves only your notes and a pointer to each photo. Small copies are uploaded only when you share by link, and deleted when you stop sharing.

Is it free?

Yes. You can record up to 300 photos and 10 trips and share them by link for free. Choose Plus for more records, PDF books or Google Drive backup.

Can people without the app see my records?

Yes. People open your link in a web browser, no app needed. You can set a password or hide exact locations.

What happens to my records when I change phones?

Sign in with the same Google or Apple account and your records are all there. With Plus Google Drive backup, your photos show up on the new phone too.

Is it available on Android?

It's on iPhone first. Android is coming.

Can I export or delete my records?

Yes. In Settings you can export all your records as a file, and delete your account and records at once.
