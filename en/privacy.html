Privacy Policy

Taegabi Studio (the "Operator") protects the personal information of people who use the Pic&Fill app (the "App") in accordance with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. The App is a private journal, not a social network: your original photos stay on your phone and we process only what is necessary.

This policy is always available in the App (Settings → Privacy Policy) and on this website. This English version is a translation for convenience; if it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we process and why

Sign-up (Sign in with Google or Apple). We receive the following from the account you choose. The App asks for no other required information.

  • Google: name, email address, Google account identifier
  • Apple: name (if you share it at first sign-in), email address (an Apple relay address if you choose Hide My Email), Apple ID identifier

Only when you share by link do we upload reduced-size copies, and we delete them when you stop sharing or the share expires.

We do not infer your interests or preferences for personalized advertising.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Account and records: until you delete your account (Settings → Delete account); deleted immediately.
  • Shared photos: deleted when sharing stops or expires.
  • Inquiry emails: deleted within one year after we reply. Feedback sent in the App is deleted with your account.
  • Crash logs: removed after the retention period of the crash reporting service (Firebase Crashlytics, about 90 days).
  • Where other laws require retention, we keep the information separately for that period.

Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be restored. We do not keep personal information on paper.

3. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide your personal information to other companies or people, except content you share by link (visible to anyone with the link) and requests from authorities made under legal procedures.

5. Advertising

Free users may choose to watch an ad (Google AdMob rewarded ads) before using some features. We request non-personalized ads only. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. Google may process device and ad display information to show ads and prevent fraud. Plus users see no ads.

6. Google Drive backup (Plus)

If you turn it on, reduced-size copies of your photos are uploaded to your own Google Drive. The App uses the narrowest permission (only files the App created), and the Operator cannot see these files. Backups remain in your Drive even after you delete your account.

7. Phone permissions

Photos (importing, reading photo information), Camera (taking photos in the App), Location (saving the place when you take a photo in the App, only while in use), Notifications (note and memory reminders), Microphone and Speech Recognition (voice notes), Face ID (app lock), Paste (adding links). All are optional; the rest of the App works without them. You can change them anytime in iPhone Settings → Pic&Fill.

8. Cookies

The App does not use cookies. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and button clicks, which may store cookies in your browser. It does not use information that identifies you personally.

To refuse, block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The website still works with cookies blocked.

9. Your rights

You (or a legal guardian for a child under 14) may access, correct, delete, or ask us to stop processing your personal information at any time.

  • View, edit and delete: directly in the App.
  • Export everything: Settings → Export my records.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
  • No longer have the app? Email "Delete account" to picandfill@taegabi.com from the address you signed up with. We delete your account and all records within 7 days and let you know.
  • Other requests: email us and we will respond without delay.

10. Children

The App is intended for people aged 14 and over. Information about a child entered in the "Half" feature is treated as the record of the guardian who uses the App.

11. Security

  • All connections between the App and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Server access rules allow each user to read and write only their own records.
  • Share passwords are stored only as irreversible hashes, with temporary lockout after repeated failures.
  • Only the Operator has server administration access, kept to the minimum necessary.

12. Privacy officer

  • Privacy officer: 이현배 (CEO, Taegabi Studio)

You may also contact the Korean authorities: Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118), Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

13. Scope

This policy applies to the Pic&Fill app, shared web pages (picandfill.web.app) and this website. Other companies' services opened from the App (Google Maps, YouTube, etc.) follow their own policies.

14. Changes

We will announce changes in the App or on this page 7 days before they take effect (30 days if the change is unfavorable to you).
